# Assistant teaching brief

Criteria version: `limassol-calm-value-2026-09-30`. Declared by the user on September 30, 2026.

The assistant may inspect newly imported adverts and supply its own reviews. These labels are marked `agent`; they never count as human choices. A human rating takes priority. Each review stores every ordered photo hash, one observation per photo, the listing fields, location evidence, decision, reasons and unknowns. Changed photos, fields, road evidence or removal make the displayed shortlist review stale.

## What to favour

- Consistently fresh, contemporary renovation throughout the living rooms, bedrooms, kitchen and bathrooms. A newly posted advert is not proof of a newly built apartment.
- Calm white, grey, wood and other neutral finishes. Reject dominant red kitchen fronts, feature walls or large furnishings. Red clothing, cars outside or tiny unrelated objects are not red interiors.
- Visible contemporary oven, hob, refrigerator, washing machine and climate equipment. A photo establishes appearance and presence, not working condition, exact age, brand quality or energy efficiency. Never invent hidden integrated appliances.
- Useful open floor area and a substantial balcony or loggia. Check the entire gallery, not only the hero photo. Unknown balcony size or shelter remains unknown.
- Reasonable rent per **stated** square metre. The current initial search uses 60–180 m² and at most €23/m²/month, within the saved €2,800 ceiling. This is a candidate filter, not a claim about fair market value. The advert may include veranda space in its area; comparisons remain approximate unless internal area is specified.
- Preserve the existing minimum bedroom count, covered-parking requirement and excluded areas.

## Review decisions and learning

- `+1`: a supported shortlist candidate passing the saved brief and location rule. Every unique photo contributes to bounded positive KC→MBON reinforcement.
- `0`: promising but insufficient evidence or a brief conflict needing confirmation. No positive reinforcement.
- `−1`: clearly inconsistent renovation or dominant red interiors. Updates the preference readout; an aversive dopamine circuit is still unimplemented.

The assistant evaluates photos at their available resolution. **Pure Fly** keeps its existing low-resolution sensory adapter; no semantic vision model is silently inserted into its brain. Assistant inspection supplies supervision, not extra sensory channels. Pure's luminance pooling cannot directly preserve red hue or detailed appliance semantics. Held-out assistant labels measure imitation of this brief, not agreement with the user's own taste.

## Measurement discipline

First publish training reviews and let memory finish. Freeze a new, previously unreviewed apartment cohort before saving its labels. Hold its photos and labels out of reinforcement and readout fitting. Freeze model design, checkpoint, gallery hashes, brief and mode. Report assistant and human label counts separately. A small, curated cohort cannot establish general recommendation quality or fulfil the target of 50 genuine human choices.
